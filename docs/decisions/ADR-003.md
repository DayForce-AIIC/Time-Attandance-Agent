# ADR-003: Structured policy retrieval

## Context
The challenge provides a small, fixed set of rules and jurisdiction notes.

## Decision
Store policy passages in `policies.json`; retrieve the violated rule followed by up to two explicitly related passages. Do not add a vector database or orchestration framework.

## Consequences
Retrieval is offline, deterministic, inspectable, and easy to test. A larger policy corpus may need a different retrieval layer, but that is outside this prototype.
